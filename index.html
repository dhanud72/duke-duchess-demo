Get the app

Install the app.

Put New Duke & Duchess on your home screen. Book in two taps, free, no app store, and it opens even with a weak signal.

✓ You’re using the app. Nothing more to do.

Your device

Android phone or tablet

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap Install. The icon appears on your home screen.
Your device

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The icon appears on your home screen.
Your device

Windows computer

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. No icon? Chrome: ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app. Edge: ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app.
  4. Click Install. It opens in its own window and sits on the Start menu.
Your device

Mac

  1. Safari: open this page, then File → Add to Dock, then Add.
  2. Chrome: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install.
  3. The app opens in its own window and stays in the Dock and Launchpad.
Your device

Linux or Chromebook

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. No icon? ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app.
  4. Click Install. It appears with your other apps.
