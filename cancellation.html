Cancellation & rescheduling policy

Draft for the salon to review. The 4-hour window below matches the demo and can be changed in the admin settings.

Change or cancel online

Sign in to My account and choose Reschedule or Cancel up to 4 hours before your appointment. Your slot is released at once, and anyone on the waitlist is told.

Closer than 4 hours

Please call the branch on +91 89047 10353. We will do our best to help, but cannot always move a slot at short notice.

Paid online

When you cancelWhat happens
More than 4 hours beforeFull refund to the original payment method in 5–7 working days.
Less than 4 hours before, or no-show[Salon to decide: for example, the amount is kept or converted to salon credit.]

Paid at the salon

Nothing to refund. Repeated no-shows may mean we ask for online payment on future bookings.

Passes, gift cards and points

If you cancel in time, the pass session, gift card balance and loyalty points used on that booking are returned to you.

If we cancel

If we have to cancel (for example a stylist is unwell), we will message you, offer another time and refund anything you paid online in full.
