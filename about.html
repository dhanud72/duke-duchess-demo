About us

Two studios, one standard.

Draft. The salon should add its own story (who started it, the team, what it is proud of). Everything below is taken from what is true of the two studios today.

New Duke & Duchess is a unisex salon with two studios in Bengaluru: Rajarajeshwari Nagar and Nagarabhavi. Both are open every day from 9 AM to 10 PM and look after men, women and children.

What we do

  • Hair: haircuts and styling, colouring and highlights, keratin treatment, hair spa, beard grooming, kids’ haircuts.
  • Skin: facials and skin consultations.
  • Nails: manicures and pedicures.
  • Bridal & makeup: bridal grooming, makeup and complete makeovers.

How we work

Tools are sanitised and every chair has a spotless workstation. You can choose your stylist or let us pick, see only the times that are really free, and change a booking online up to four hours before your visit.

The studios

Rajarajeshwari Nagar has marble floors, round LED mirrors, black styling chairs and a calm green skin-care suite. Nagarabhavi has gold-framed mirrors, quiet treatment rooms and a pedicure lounge, with a round reception desk under the Duke & Duchess mural.
