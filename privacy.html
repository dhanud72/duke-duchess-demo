Who we are

New Duke & Duchess is a unisex salon with studios in Rajarajeshwari Nagar and Nagarabhavi, Bengaluru. We are the data fiduciary for the information described here, under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Booking details: name, mobile number, email (optional), the service, stylist, date, time and any notes you add.
  • Account details if you sign in: your name and email from Google, or your mobile number, plus your loyalty points, passes and gift cards.
  • Order details: products ordered, pickup branch and payment status. We never see or store your card or UPI details; the payment provider handles them.
  • Reviews you choose to write.
  • Technical data such as pages visited, only if you accept analytics cookies.

Why we use it

To confirm and manage your appointments and orders, send confirmations and reminders by email, WhatsApp or SMS, run loyalty points, passes and gift cards, answer your questions, prevent misuse, and meet legal duties. With your consent we also use analytics to improve the site. We do not sell your data.
