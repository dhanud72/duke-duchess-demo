Update your website.

Everything on the website comes from one Google Sheet. Change the sheet on your phone or computer, and every customer sees it the next time they open the site.

1. Your sheet

Open the Google Sheet ↗

2. What each tab changes

One row per item. In the Branches column write R R Nagar, Nagarabhavi or Both. To hide something without deleting it, write no in Show.

Services

  • Name, Category (Hair, Skin, Nails or Bridal), Duration in minutes, Price as you want it shown (₹350 or ₹2,999 onwards).
  • Booking times use the duration, so keep it accurate.

Team

  • Name, Role, Experience, About, Branches.
  • Services: the services this person does, written exactly as in the Services tab, separated by commas.
  • Photo link: see “Adding photos” below.

Products

  • Price in numbers only, like 1299. Leave it empty to show “Price at salon”.
  • In stock: no shows “Out of stock” and stops orders.

Offers

  • Title, Discount (20% off), Details, Branches.
  • Valid until: a date like 30/11/2026. The offer disappears by itself after that day.

Hours

  • One row per day, one column per branch: 9:00 AM - 10:00 PM, or Closed for a weekly off.

Holidays

  • Date (and Until, for several days), Branches, Reason (customers see it, like Diwali).
  • Open for a half day? Put the hours in Special hours, like 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM. Leave it empty to close for the day.

Settings

  • WhatsApp and phone number for each branch. Bookings and orders go to these.
  • Booking slot (minutes between times), minimum notice, how many days ahead people can book.
  • Announcement: a message shown at the top of the website, with an end date in Announcement until.

3. Adding photos

  1. Open the Google Drive app and upload the photo.
  2. iPhone: tap ⋯ on the photo → Share → Manage access, set General access to Anyone with the link. Android: tap ⋮ → Share → Manage access → Anyone with the link.
  3. Tap Copy link, open the sheet in the Google Sheets app, and paste it in the Photo link column.

Use a clear, bright photo, taken in portrait. One person per photo for the Team tab.

4. Bookings and orders

Customers send them to you on WhatsApp, already written out. Reply to confirm the slot or tell them when an order is ready.

Need a change the sheet can’t make (new photos of the salon, page layout)? Ask your web developer.
