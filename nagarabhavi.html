Questions, answered

Can I book online?

Yes. Choose a branch, a service, a stylist (or “any available”), then a day and time. Only times that are really free are shown, and your confirmation arrives by email and WhatsApp straight away.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. Sign in to My account and reschedule or cancel up to 4 hours before your visit. Closer than that, please call the branch.

Do I need an account to book?

No. You can book as a guest with your name and mobile number. Signing in (Google or a mobile code) lets you see your bookings, orders, passes and loyalty points in one place.

How do I pay?

Pay at the salon, or pay online when you book or order (UPI, cards, net banking and wallets). Gift cards, passes and loyalty points can cover all or part of a bill.

Can I buy salon products online?

Yes. Add products to your bag, order, and collect them at the branch you choose. We do not deliver; the team hands your order over at the salon.

How do gift cards work?

Choose an amount from ₹100 to ₹20,000. The card is sent to you by WhatsApp and email with a code, which you can share. Enter the code when you book or check out.

What are passes and loyalty points?

A pass bundles 4 or 5 services at a lower price, valid for 180 days. Loyalty points give you 1 point for every ₹10 you spend; each point is worth ₹1 off, up to 20% of a bill.

Are both branches open at the same times?

Both Rajarajeshwari Nagar and Nagarabhavi are open every day from 9:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Festival and special hours are shown on the website before they happen.

Do you serve men, women and children?

Yes. Haircuts, colour, beard grooming, kids’ cuts, hair spa, facials, skin consultations, manicures, pedicures, bridal grooming and makeup, for men, women and kids.

How do you keep things clean?

Tools are sanitised and every chair has a spotless workstation, in both studios.

How is my personal information used?

Only to run your booking, orders and reminders. We never sell it. Read the privacy policy for the details, your rights and how to ask us to delete your data.
